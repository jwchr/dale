## What the rider gets

Dale gives riders a small daily report on routes 163, 164, 165 and 165T, both directions: buses seen running, the busiest observed moment, late buses and the worst delay, rush-hour and weather comparisons, and eventually miles with a fun comparison. A running record starts at the first processed date and names missing days. It helps explain the trip into the city and home without crowding the next-bus information. These are sampled past observations, not official performance scores or a promise about the next bus. Train stats wait until usable rail history and matching schedules are collected and verified.

## Government weather source

Use NOAA NCEI GHCN-Daily via its [public Access Data Service](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/support/access-data-service-api-user-documentation): `daily-summaries`, station `USW00014734`, `PRCP,SNOW`, metric units and quality attributes. The documented GET uses no account/key. NOAA identifies this as [Newark Liberty International Airport](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=daily-summaries&endDate=2025-04-30&format=pdf&startDate=2025-04-01&stations=USW00014734). This is one regional proxy, not weather along every bus route.

Proposed “bad weather” class is precipitation >=6.35 mm (0.25 inch) OR snow >=25.4 mm (1 inch); “normal” requires both valid daily measurements below those thresholds. Missing/failed-quality values stay unknown, including missing snow on a rainy day below the rain threshold. Label the class “wet/snowy days,” disclose these Dale thresholds, and credit NOAA. It does not cover every wind, ice or visibility hazard. [GHCN documentation](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/readme.txt) defines raw PRCP in tenths of mm and SNOW in mm; do not apply raw-file scaling again to converted API values.

Docs checked November 26; an attempted dated JSON request failed in the browsing tool. Current anonymous delivery, converted values, station coverage, publication lag, and the reporting day's alignment with Eastern dates/DST are **not checked**. Verify those before enabling weather comparisons; never blindly join date strings. Fetch at most one bounded 31-day station window per daily job with a 5-second timeout and 256 KB cap. Retry unavailable dates in later daily jobs; keep weather pending independently of transit. No NJ Transit request is added.
