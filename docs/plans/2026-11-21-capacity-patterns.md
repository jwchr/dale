# Capacity observations and labeled past answers (#33)

Jeff approved building the remaining bounded work on Nov 21, 2026. This plan uses repository evidence only, with no network calls or recorded feed responses. Shared reports and patterns across riders depend on unbuilt, unapproved #64 and are outside this plan.

## What exists today

- `src/domain/busLock.ts` and `src/ui/BusLockPanel.tsx` accept explicit seats/standing/looked-full answers after arrival, boarding or a pass-up. Reports carry action time, trip instance (including service date) and boarding stop. They do not prove a vehicle assignment when no official vehicle is locked.
- #57 / PR #74's `src/domain/reportHistory.ts` and `src/ui/PastReports.tsx` already save the rider's own answers locally, exclude the selected service date/session, and count the last capacity answer per session. Matching uses route, stop, scheduled minute (including hours beyond 24) and weekday/Saturday/Sunday. Storage is capped at 1,000 reports and 180 days, with a clear-history boundary; demo reports are excluded.
- The existing card shows total rides, an overall date range and category counts. It lacks a capacity-only denominator/date range, last-answer age and past schedule context. `PastReport` has no direction, pattern, trip, vehicle or feed-version field. Its matching cannot prove an unchanged exact scheduled service.
- `docs/HANDOFF.md` reports the bus pilot and private collection live. That is a dated repository statement, not live verification in this task. No occupancy feature or production occupancy sample is established here.

## Data source and proof

- `worker/njt-client.ts` already calls authorized GTFSG2 `getVehiclePositions` and `getTripUpdates` with existing server credentials. `docs/BUS_API_SETUP.md` proves the documented authentication contract, not occupancy availability. No new API, key, account, scraping or paid source is needed or proposed.
- `worker/normalize.ts` decodes through the maintained `gtfs-realtime-bindings` (2.2.0, documented in `docs/LIVE_BUS_PILOT.md`). It never reads `occupancyStatus` / GTFS-RT `occupancy_status`, and its observation/presence types do not carry it. The generated dependency bindings are not vendored in the researched source; their enum/presence behavior must be checked with synthetic encoding/decoding before implementation relies on them. Binding support alone would not prove NJ Transit populates the field.
- `worker/generated/` is preparation output for schedules/geometry, not evidence of live occupancy. `scripts/prepare-live-worker.mjs` does not establish occupancy availability.
- `worker/history-evidence.ts` copies an explicit GPS/stop-call whitelist; `worker/history-journal.ts` separately whitelists diagnostics and census fields. `docs/HISTORY_COLLECTION.md` preserves normalized evidence/context, not complete raw GTFS-RT messages. Occupancy discarded before these stages cannot be recovered from existing archives. Do not backfill invented occupancy or treat the word “originals” as proof that every upstream field survived.
- `docs/BUS_LOCK.md` mentions MyBus occupancy categories, but explicitly calls authorized-feed availability unverified. **Usable NJ Transit occupancy data is unproven in this repository.** A count-only diagnostic is the next evidence step, not a rider crowd estimate or a claim about the whole fleet.

## Requirements and reasons

- Follow `CLAUDE.md` “Product rules” and “Data and privacy,” `AGENTS.md`, and `docs/V1_CONTRACT.md`: source and age on every item, unknown as a valid answer, no silent bus replacement, and every rider string in English and Spanish. Reason: observations must not become invented live facts.
- Keep today's explicit answer separate from prior service dates. Show its original observation time and advancing age, selected trip context, and whether the vehicle match is known; never assign a pending replacement silently. Reason: a new poll or screen visit is not a new rider observation.
- Label history “Your past answers on this phone” / “Tus respuestas anteriores en este teléfono.” Show capacity sample count, capacity-only first/last service dates, latest answer time/age and each category's count; zero answers means unknown, not empty. Reason: arrival-only rides cannot inflate the crowding sample.
- Describe saved answers, without predicting today's load, occupancy percentages or PABT queue length. A pass-up is a separate event, not proof of fullness. Reason: bus occupancy, service events and waiting lines measure different things.
- Retain existing route/stop/slot/day-type grouping, clearly described as those recorded attributes. Where several current directions/patterns share them, suppress the comparison as ambiguous. Surface relevant existing `commuteChanges` notices; do not infer an unchanged service from no notice or an unchanged departure minute. Reason: schedule rollovers, detours, holidays and service changes can break comparability.
- Legacy answers have no historical schedule context: disclose “Past schedule/service changes unknown” / “Cambios anteriores de horario o servicio desconocidos.” Keep them as descriptive personal answers, never a verified same-service typical pattern; do not silently join changed IDs, directions or express variants. Reason: no new data or retrospective identity may be fabricated.
- Use existing fields and existing schedule-change information only. Preserve retention, clear-history behavior, local storage failure handling and demo isolation; add no report uploads, identifiers or collection. Reason: this slice improves the rider's own history without expanding data collection.

Keep only the latest poll aggregate; persist its count-only quality in the existing journal batch/transaction if collection is already enabled, with no extra SQL write or new archive object. The aggregate is fixed-size (fixed enum keys, counts and clocks; budget at most 512 bytes per poll, under 1.5 MB/day across 2,880 polls, inside the 250 MB journal payload guard). If adding it would push a batch past 128,000 bytes, drop the diagnostic quality for that poll and record the drop as a fixed `occupancyDropped: true` flag in that poll's own persisted journal quality row (a few bytes, counted inside the 128,000-byte cap, same transaction, no extra SQL write; never only in health memory, which a Worker restart loses). Never drop or fail core arrival/GPS evidence for it. Readers treat older journal rows without occupancy fields and without that flag as "not recorded", never as zero, and keep dropped polls separate from them.
5. Extend journal quality whitelists explicitly. Counts and completed-attempt acknowledgment commit together; retries/cache reads must not count a poll twice, and a crash cannot acknowledge evidence that was not inserted. Respect existing payload/storage/write reservations, archive-before-prune and immutable conditional-create/readback behavior. Do not rewrite old archives or enable a collector to obtain diagnostic counts.
6. After a separately approved diagnostic deployment with collection already enabled, report, from a read-only aggregation over the persisted journal quality rows, the bounded sample's dates, poll count, dropped-diagnostic poll count (with their times of day, since large batches cluster at rush hour), source age, missing fraction and observed categories as totals only. Dropped polls are reported as missing sample, never folded into "not recorded" or silently excluded. If collection is off, there is no multi-poll sample: report occupancy unavailable rather than scraping the latest-poll health value. This task runs no diagnostic. Positive counts prove only field presence in that sample; all-missing or invalid values leave occupancy unavailable. Exact trip/vehicle/time validity and category meaning remain unproven for rider delivery. Rider-facing official occupancy and new occupancy history need a follow-up evidence-based plan and owner-approved release, not automatic activation.
