## What exists today and what the sources prove

- **Schedule replacement is built:** `docs/SCHEDULE_NOTICES.md` (#151/#162), `src/domain/commuteChanges.ts`, `src/data/commuteChangeStore.ts` and `src/ui/CommuteChanges.tsx` compare saved trips across official schedule versions. First load establishes a baseline; unread notices persist locally until dismissed. `docs/HANDOFF.md` reports this live; that is repository evidence, not a fresh production check.
- **Official calendar data exists in the app:** `scripts/gtfs-lib.mjs` imports `calendar.txt` and `calendar_dates.txt` into service ranges and additions/removals. `src/domain/schedule.ts` applies them with pickup/drop-off, direction and service-date rules. `src/data/liveSchedule.ts` loads the existing `/v1/schedule` and saves an offline copy with its saved time.
- **Source clocks exist, with limits:** `src/domain/types.ts` has source, feed version and `builtAt`. Build time is not NJ Transit's publication time. Existing notice records retain versions, but need per-notice source-time metadata to show age after a baseline advances.
- **Printed holiday warnings exist:** `src/domain/holidays.ts` identifies the printed guide's warning dates, not holiday departure times. GTFS calendars prove scheduled service for covered dates, not the reason for a calendar exception or current disruptions.
- **Gate logic exists, live gate changes do not:** `src/domain/gate.ts` supports posted/published/unknown answers and change detection, but `src/ui/App.tsx` passes no real postings. Printed rules are not posted assignments. The dated construction advisory in `docs/HANDOFF.md` and the manual travel-alerts link in `src/ui/PennGuide.tsx` do not prove an alerts integration.
- **Alerts endpoint and entitlement are unproven:** `worker/njt-client.ts` supports only authentication, static GTFS, vehicle positions and trip updates. `docs/BUS_API_SETUP.md` proves documented bus authentication, not alerts access; `docs/LIVE_BUS_PILOT.md` explicitly excludes service alerts. No exact GTFS-RT alerts or advisories RSS endpoint is established by the searched client/docs. Do not guess one or treat the website link as a feed.

## Requirements and rider behavior

- Follow `CLAUDE.md` **Product rules**: never invent live facts, preserve the exact-bus lock, never infer a gate from a route, and provide every string in English and Spanish with 12-hour times. Missing reports mean not reporting, never cancelled.
- One compact briefing beside the saved commute, extending the existing notices rather than adding a second notice store. Show selected route/variant, boarding stop, direction and travel date/time window. Use the rider's current selection; do not invent a usual commute time. Keep both legs distinguishable and favorites in their order.
- Each item contains the supported change or uncertainty, applicable scope/time, source, age, and a concrete action. Show source publication/update time when supplied, last successful check separately, and validity dates separately. Unknown source age stays explicitly unknown; receipt time never makes old content fresh.
- Retain build/version age for schedules, printed issue/effective dates for printed rules, and offline saved-copy age when applicable. Add optional metadata to the existing validated notice record without resetting notices or bypassing its atomic/cross-tab safeguards; older records show unknown age.
- Timetable replacement reuses the existing comparison and before/after details. A new file alone is not proof every trip changed; missing overlap or a missing saved selection gets the existing uncertainty/reselect action, never a cancellation claim. Link to the full timetable for the chosen date.
- Holiday service uses the selected date's actual GTFS calendar and matching departures. Do not assume Sunday service, infer a holiday label from an exception, or claim a missing date means no service. Covered scheduled no-service and unavailable/expired coverage must remain distinct. Printed holiday dates warn that holiday times are unavailable and offer the existing official-check link.
- Always show **"Couldn't check current alerts" / "No se pudieron consultar las alertas actuales"** while the source is unverified, disabled, offline, stale, denied, malformed or unreachable. Include attempted-check time if any; without a successful check, say so. Keep known schedule changes visible beside this state.
- Only a fresh successful check of a verified source with known coverage may say no matching alerts, qualified by scope and check age. Cached notices remain labeled last known; expiry, disappearance or a failed fetch never proves a closure ended. No automatic route, stop, bus or gate switch.
- Future detours/closures must match documented route/stop/direction selectors and active periods against the selected journey, preserving broader agency/route scope where appropriate. Ambiguous selectors stay uncertain. Never invent an alternate boarding stop or walking route; advise checking the official notice/stop sign or asking the driver when no replacement is supplied.
- A future gate-change item needs an official assignment for the exact trip/service date, its source timestamp and a supported prior gate. Published rules stay labeled published; otherwise show gate unconfirmed and advise checking the board. Alerts access alone does not establish gate access.
- All headings, statuses, source/age labels, actions, accessibility text and notice summaries need EN/ES. Use source-supported bilingual facts and reviewed Dale translations labeled as such; if an agency text cannot be rendered reliably in both languages, show a bilingual unavailable summary and source link. Render external content as text, never arbitrary HTML or guessed instructions.

## Privacy, free limits and NJ Transit terms

- Apply `CLAUDE.md` **Data and privacy**, `docs/LIVE_BUS_PILOT.md`, `docs/HISTORY_COLLECTION.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md` D73. Existing NJ Transit credentials are allowed server-side only. No new key, account, vendor or paid service; never record feed responses for this work.

Every alerts or gate request is fixed-scope (corridor-wide, the same URL for every rider): no stop, route, trip, service date, time window or commute parameter, header or body, so Worker and Cloudflare logs never hold one rider's commute. The phone filters that payload to its saved commutes locally. No GPS, rider identity, vehicle IDs, secrets, feed recordings or personal data in this public repo, logs or new public responses. Expose only sanitized advisory facts needed for the briefing; public history remains aggregate totals only, with no raw-history endpoint.
- Terms evidence is the repository's dated D73 account: attribute NJ Transit, identify Dale's relay, disclose latency beyond one minute, make no accuracy/completeness/timeliness or endorsement claim. Alerts-specific reuse/translation terms and entitlement remain unproven and must be established before ingestion; a free website is not proof of feed rights.
- Before adding alerts calls, budget all existing consumers, authentication, retries and the proposed refresh cadence against NJ Transit's 40,000 requests/day per API and the documented shared Cloudflare Free guards. The existing two-feed 30-second loop is at most 5,760 feed calls/day plus bounded authentication, not spare capacity automatically granted to alerts. Reserve shared budget before I/O, coalesce/cache demand, bound bodies/timeouts, refuse redirects and back off failures; exhaustion returns unavailable, never paid overflow.
- Reuse the documented persistent SQL/R2/write/operation guards; do not relax them or introduce an alerts archive. History/context transactions and idempotent checkpoints must survive crash/retry without loss or double counting. Existing immutable archive objects/manifests must never be overwritten or deleted; hot cleanup still requires verified durable readback. Keep cap values in `docs/HISTORY_COLLECTION.md`, their single home.
